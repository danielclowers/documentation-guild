## Assumptions and reward rules

- All three players use Scribing. A five-minute connected cycle earns 3 character XP, 5 skill XP and 3 gold. Research and Restoration have the same XP rates and earn 2 gold. Across 60 days, either alternative standing order would earn 2,112 gold for the casual schedule or 8,448 for the typical schedule, before other activities. Time away and time on expedition grant zero standing-order progress. The model keeps partial cycles and assumes regular payouts. Chronicler, available at Level 10, rounds down 10% of the gold in each settlement. At one Scribing cycle per settlement it adds zero gold. The table uses that ordinary payout pattern.
- The engaged player completes four cases from the 12-case rotation per weekday and selects the +4 gold option where offered. Intro and normal cases pay 20 XP, 10 skill XP and 12 base gold. Advanced cases pay 35 XP, 16 skill XP and 20 base gold. The model assumes at most two wrong guesses on any case. Three or more wrong guesses reduce the full payout to 75% before rounding. Four daily cases stay below the eight full rewards per rolling 24 hours. Additional cases in that window award 5 XP and 2 gold, with zero skill XP. Cases are completed during the connected session, including while the expedition runs.
- The engaged player purchases Efficient Scribing ranks at the first eligible daily check after reaching Level 3 and affording each rank. The three ranks cost 650 gold total and add 3 percentage points each to Scribing case gold. The player chooses Editor at the first eligible daily check after reaching Level 10. Editor adds 15 points, for a combined 24% on those cases under the 25% cap. Each case rounds down. These purchases leave about 17,350 gold by day 60 if there is no other spending. Mender offers a separate +10% Restoration case-gold build. The Research and Restoration specializations affect optional expedition outcomes and discoveries, so those builds would require their own activity assumptions.
- The engaged player can find at least one other player each weekday. They complete the 10-minute tutorial first, then a 20-minute Chapter 1 standard mission at recommended level 2. Tutorial, standard and advanced missions award 60/110/140 character XP, 20/35/45 role skill XP and 35/60/80 gold per participant. Shared renown and discoveries add no character XP or gold. The model deducts expedition minutes from connected Scribing time.
- Expected optional payouts use one tutorial challenge and an average of 1.5 per later expedition. The player takes the careful approach with a +5 percentage point modifier and chooses the reliable option with a +10 point modifier. Success odds use the evolving skill XP for the event's skill, weighted across the catalog's 8 Scribing, 9 Research and 7 Restoration events, with the implemented 30–95% bounds. Each successful event averages 15 XP and 10 gold before Expedition Readiness. The main scenario buys only Efficient Scribing, so no Expedition Readiness modifier applies. Actual results depend on the events and rolls served.
- If Chapter 2 is open and an eligible party runs an advanced mission from workday 11 onward, the engaged forecast rises to about 22,645 XP and 18,650 gold by day 60, with Level 20 around day 36. At eight full-reward cases per weekday and the standard expedition schedule, the forecast is about 25,671 XP and 20,756 gold by day 60, with Level 20 around day 31. These estimates assume consistent daily case timing so each day's full-reward quota is available.

## Balance questions to revisit after playtesting

- Connected standing orders contribute about 56% of the engaged player's XP and 68% of their gold by day 60. Eight-hour schedules reach the cap about four times faster than two-hour schedules on standing orders alone. Session length drives much of the difference between players with different work schedules.
- The engaged player reaches Level 20 about 24 calendar days before the typical player and earns about 8,808 XP beyond the cap by day 60. XP continues to accrue after the cap. The three modelled training purchases absorb only 650 of roughly 18,000 gross gold. Buying all 12 training ranks would cost 2,600 gold at the current prices. Track how much gold capped characters retain after training and specialization changes.
- Chronicler's rounding makes its value depend on settlement timing. One to three Scribing cycles per payout yield zero bonus gold, while four cycles yield one extra gold. The specialization can feel inactive during regular play.
- The Business quota lowers payouts after eight full cases. It still permits continued 5 XP and 2 gold case rewards. Expeditions have no daily quota and can be repeated once a party returns. Compare these forecasts with actual case volume, party availability and repeats before deciding whether either activity is producing more progression than intended.
